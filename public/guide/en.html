AI checks availability and drafts a reply. The agent reviews the wording and sends.
03

01

Every enquiry in one inbox

Unified Inbox & WhatsApp Parser

Messages from WhatsApp, email (Gmail) and the traveller portal collect in one inbox. WhatsApp is two-way, so client replies arrive automatically. An AI parser reads the conversation, pulls out the details a quote needs, and hands them straight to itinerary building.

Working the inbox
  1. Open WhatsApp Inbox in the sidebar — conversations on the left, the open chat on the right.
  2. Pick a conversation, type your reply at the bottom, and Send.
  3. As needed: assign to an agent, use quick replies, set the reply language, and link or create a client record.
  4. Send quotes, invoices, contracts and receipts straight from the conversation.
Turn a conversation into a quote
  1. From the dashboard, click Parse WhatsApp (or WhatsApp Parser in the sidebar).
  2. Paste the conversation text and click Parse.
  3. Review the extracted details and click Create Itinerary — a priced itinerary is generated.
What the AI extracts
Name · email · phoneNationality · passport typeTravel dates Pax (adults · children · infants)Destinations · interestsBudget rangeSpecial requests
Every extracted field carries a confidence score, so you can see how sure the AI is. Pasting a conversation is the fastest way to turn an enquiry into a quote.
02

AI drafts the reply, a human approves it

WhatsApp AI Agent & Copilot

Before writing, the WhatsApp AI agent looks up your real data (tool-calling). What it produces is always a draft suggestion, saved against the conversation and waiting for approval. The agent never sends on its own. The feature is off by default and enabled per organisation.

What the agent can look up (read-only)
Search customer trips
The linked client's itineraries and bookings, with dates and status.
Look up an itinerary
The day-by-day detail and pricing of one specific trip.
Check availability
Capacity for the dates and any group departures in range. It won't promise dates you can't serve.
Escalate to a human
Complaints, cancellations, refunds, money or anything urgent go to a person instead of a guess.
Each suggestion shows a confidence signal and its reasoning (which tools it used). Sensitive messages — a complaint, a refund, a same-day request — are escalated rather than answered.
AI Copilot — grounded in your own knowledge (RAG)

Incoming messages gather in a shared Copilot inbox; the assistant retrieves the most relevant facts you've taught it and proposes a draft. Everything is approval-gated — a human always sends. Manage what it knows in Copilot Knowledge.

FAQPolicy (cancellation · refunds)Tour descriptionsOther
Agent memory: the system learns from your approved replies and work patterns (client preferences, pricing patterns, supplier notes) and feeds them into itinerary generation — so suggestions sound more like you over time. Nothing to configure.
03

Build the itinerary and quote

Quote Builder & Itineraries

The Quote Builder is Autoura's single pricing engine. Drop services into fixed day slots and the whole trip prices in real time. The same engine powers both B2C quotes and B2B rate sheets, so the numbers always agree.

Three ways to start
  1. Paste text — from WhatsApp, email or operator shorthand; AI reads the cities, activities and services into a day-by-day grid.
  2. Upload a file — PDF, image (PNG/JPG/WebP) or DOCX, read by vision into the same grid.
  3. Load / build — enter an itinerary ID to reprice an existing trip, or click “+” to add days and build from scratch.
The Trip Settings bar drives every calculation
Pax
Affects vehicle selection, per-person cost and accommodation splits.
Start date
Sets the seasonal (dated-period) rates for hotels and cruises.
Passport type
EU or non-EU — entrance fees and some services differ.
Tier
Budget / Standard / Deluxe / Luxury — which hotels and service levels are used.
Pricing mode
B2C (direct) or B2B (partner). B2B enables partner selection and rate sheets.
Currency
EUR / USD / GBP / EGP / JPY — all rates converted and displayed.
Margin %
Applied to total cost to produce the selling price.
Change any setting and it recalculates instantly — cost per person, total cost, margin, sell per person and sell total.
Up to 14 service slots per day

AI maps each service to the best rate in your database — you only edit the slots it guessed wrong or where you want a different option. A day-type preset (arrival, tour, transfer, cruise, free, departure) fills sensible defaults.

Children (4–12) are 50% off, infants (0–3) free except flights — the pax mix is reflected in pricing. Keep your rates current and Auto mode prices every trip instantly and accurately.
04

How auto-pricing works

Rate Management & Auto-Pricing

The engine sorts costs into three kinds, so pricing stays correct as the group size changes.

Fixed costs
Same regardless of pax — guide, tipping, airport services. Split across the group.
Per-person costs
Scale with pax — accommodation (per-night), entrance fees, meals, water, Nile cruise.
Transport costs
Step up with group size: sedan → minivan → van → minibus → bus, chosen automatically.
Built for Egypt handling
16 rate categories
Hotels, Nile cruises, sleeper trains, flights, entrance fees, guides, transport, tipping and more.
Dated rate periods
Hotels and cruises take as many dated price periods as the contract has.
Dual passport rates
EU / non-EU fees applied automatically per service.
Cruise cabins
Priced per person per night by cabin (single/double/triple/suite); the cheapest valid combination is chosen.
Coverage checks
Any unpriced service is flagged before the quote goes out — a blank rate is never read as “free”.
Transport is the one cost that isn't linear in group size. The grid switches vehicle class per pax count for you — no manual maths.
05

B2B quotes & revision history

B2B Quotes & Revisions

B2B pricing calculates per-person rates across 1–40 pax at once, with or without a tour leader (+0 / +1) and single-supplement included — the rate sheet partners expect.

Generate a rate sheet
  1. Choose a tour variation, then set pax, travel date, passport type and margin.
  2. Click Calculate Price — summary, single supplement, and a day-by-day cost breakdown.
  3. Set the pax range From–To (1–40) and Generate Rate Sheet: cost / margin / selling / per-person / supplement for each size.
  4. Export CSV to share, or Save as Quote.
The quote (PDF) and its history
Reference code
A unique code on save; status Draft / Sent / Confirmed / Expired.
PDF export
Company branding, rate table, supplement and terms. Japanese-safe fonts for Japanese partners.
Automatic revisions
Every edit or status change snapshots the whole quote, with who changed what and when.
Compare two versions
A field-level diff showing only what changed, and how many fields.
Revert
Restore an earlier version in one step (the revert is itself recorded, so nothing is lost). Manager+.
Manage many quotes at once with bulk status updates and bulk delete (Manager role or above).
06

Bookings, departures & capacity

Operations

When a client confirms, convert the itinerary to a booking. Bookings track the operational side — supplier confirmations, payments and status.

Booking status & supplier confirmations
  1. Open the booking and click Sync from Itinerary to pull in every linked service.
  2. On the Suppliers tab, move each service Pending → Requested → Confirmed (enter the confirmation number); problems go to Issues.
  3. The booking advances Pending → Supplier Confirmed → Payment Received → Ready → In Progress → Completed.
Group departures & capacity
Create a departure
From a template or a custom tour — dates, min/max pax, price per person, booking cutoff.
Status
Draft → Open → Limited → Full → Guaranteed (min pax met) / Cancelled.
Capacity
Set “how many groups you can run” per date; availability is computed live.
Live availability
For requested dates + pax it returns Available / Limited / Busy / Blackout in customer-ready wording.
This availability also feeds the WhatsApp AI agent and resource assignment. Because the agent reads the same live numbers your team does, it never confirms a date you can't serve, and won't double-book guides or vehicles.
The calendar shows every tour in month, week and timeline views, with drag-to-reschedule and conflict detection.
07

The traveller portal

Traveller Portal

The portal is the page your customers see. Send a link and they fill in their own details, attach their passport, and ask questions — replacing the posted application form. No account, no password: the link itself is the credential.

Sending the link
  1. Open the booking and find the Customer portal panel.
  2. Choose how they fill it in: Family (one link for the whole booking) or Private (a per-traveller link others on the booking can't read).
Passport & documents
Passport
One file (a new upload replaces the old). PDF/JPEG/PNG/WEBP/HEIC (iPhone photos), 10 MB each.
Other documents
Up to six, each with a label the traveller writes (visa, insurance…).
Stored securely
Private storage; opening one issues a link that expires in five minutes. Only managers/administrators can view a scan (agents work the booking without seeing it).
Auto-deleted
Scans are destroyed automatically after the trip; the deletion date shows on each row and is fixed at upload (moving dates won't extend it).
Portal messages

Travellers ask questions from their own booking page. Before sending, they see your office hours; out of hours, they're told when the next office opens in their own time zone. The first message gets an auto-acknowledgement. Your team answers from the unified inbox's Portal tab (or the booking page). Replying emails the traveller a link back to their page — not the message text, since it may concern a passport or a payment.

Office hours are set per office (each with its own working week and time zone), so a team spread across countries is described accurately rather than averaged.
08

Invoicing, payments & finance

Finance

Raise an invoice straight from an itinerary, then track payments, balances and receivables. Multi-currency throughout, with per-trip profit & loss visible before departure.

Creating & working an invoice
  1. Open the itinerary and click Generate Invoice (all line items carry over), or create one manually.
  2. Pick a type: Standard (full) / Deposit (typically 30%) / Final (balance).
  3. The detail page shows a payment progress bar and history, with Download PDF · Send via WhatsApp/email · Add payment · Send reminder · Create final invoice.
Receipts, receivables & reports
Receipts
Generated automatically from payments (invoice or itinerary), with a receipt #, branded PDF, and WhatsApp delivery.
Receivables (AR)
Client-level aging (30/60/90 days), outstanding balances, and reminder triggers.
Profit & Loss
Supplier cost vs. client revenue — per-trip margin, visible before departure.
Financial reports
Five tabs (Overview / Revenue / Cash Flow / Tax / Commissions), with YoY, quarterly and monthly charts and CSV/PDF export.
Accounting sync (beta)
Push-only sync to QuickBooks / Xero, currently in beta.
Tax-tab VAT figures are estimates for planning — always confirm with your accountant before filing.

Foundations, roles & integrations

Beyond the workflow, the pieces that keep day-to-day operations running.

Roles & permissions (login accounts)
RoleWhat they can do
OwnerEverything, always. Transferred, never invited. The first account to sign up on a fresh install becomes owner.
AdministratorEverything, including Settings, User Management, the Activity Log and financial configuration.
ManagerOperations plus finance: rates, financial reports, receipts, commissions, P&L — but not user management or company settings.
AgentDay-to-day sales: clients, itineraries, invoices, payments, tasks, inboxes, tours, follow-ups.
ViewerDashboard only. The default for an account with no explicit role — access fails closed.
“User Management” and “Team Members” are different. User Management = who can log in and what they may do. Team Members = who does the work (task assignment, trip ownership, department). A driver who never opens Autoura is a team member with no login; give someone a login with Invite to system on their Team Members row.
